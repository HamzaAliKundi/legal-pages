Privacy Policy for Math Flash Quiz

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Math Flash Quiz ("the app") is developed by Hamza Ali ("we", "us"). This page explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, how it is used, and your choices. Please also see our Terms of Service.

1. Information we collect directly

Math Flash Quiz does not require an account, sign-in, or any personal information (name, email, phone number, etc.) to play. The only information the app stores is kept entirely on your device and is never sent to us or any server:

  • Your high score and your top scores list
  • Your sound on/off preference
  • Your daily play streak and the date you last played
  • Badges and gifts you have unlocked or opened
  • Game totals used for badges and gifts (for example, games played and correct answers)
  • Whether you turned on daily reminders
  • Whether "Remove Ads" has been bought on this device (a saved copy, so ads stay off without internet)

This data is stored locally using your device's standard app storage and is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app.

Reminders: if you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders (such as "a gift is waiting") on your device itself. They are not sent from any server, and you can turn them off in your device settings at any time.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The app displays ads using Google AdMob to keep it free. To serve ads, Google's AdMob SDK may collect certain device and advertising identifiers (such as your advertising ID) and transmit them to Google. This happens automatically as part of showing ads and is not something we directly access, store, or control.

You can learn more about how Google collects and uses this data, and your choices for controlling it, at: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services .

Purchases (Google Play Billing and RevenueCat). The app offers an optional "Remove Ads" purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details. To check whether ads should be removed, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-verification service. RevenueCat gives each install a random, anonymous ID (not your name or email) and receives purchase details from Google Play, such as which product was bought, when, and the purchase receipt. This lets the app restore your purchase on a new device. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

App updates (Expo). The app can download small updates through Expo's update service. When it checks for an update, Expo receives basic technical information needed to deliver it, such as the app version and device platform. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

3. Children's privacy

Math Flash Quiz is designed to appeal to children (approximately ages 6 to 12) and complies with the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as they apply to children.

In line with this, every ad request made by the app is tagged as "child-directed." This means:

  • Ads are not personalized or behaviorally targeted
  • Only ads rated suitable for general/family audiences (Google's "G" content rating) are shown
  • No ad formats that require account creation or purchases targeted at children are used

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the advertising identifiers and anonymous purchase records described in Section 2, which are handled by Google and RevenueCat.

The "Remove Ads" purchase sits behind a "grown-ups only" check in the app, so that a parent or guardian makes the purchase. Purchases are completed through Google Play, where parents can also use purchase approvals and parental controls.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent any personal information. Information is shared only with the services described in Section 2 (Google AdMob, Google Play, RevenueCat and Expo), and only so the app can show ads, handle purchases and receive updates.

5. Data deletion

The app does not create an account. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of the locally stored data listed in Section 1 from your device.

If you bought "Remove Ads", Google Play and RevenueCat keep a record of that purchase so it can be restored. To ask us to delete the RevenueCat record, email us (see below). Please note that deleting it may stop the purchase from being restored automatically. Your Google Play purchase history is managed by Google in your Google account.

6. Security

Game data stays on your own device. We do not operate our own servers that store or process your personal information; purchase records are handled by Google Play and RevenueCat over secure connections.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above.
